## Decision

**Build P1's chassis — exposure-abscissa monotone cumulative Hermite — with the terminal rule pinned,
grafting P2's and P3's gate discipline onto it.** Rationale, verified by the judge's own reimplementation:
P1's member closure is **algebraic, not numerical** — the member endpoints ARE interpolation knots, so the
sum telescopes exactly, independent of tangent rule, refactor, seed or convergence. P2 and P3 conserve at
~1e-11 because their solves converged; P1 conserves because it cannot not. Measured 0.0 on all 14 members
under both tangent arms and across 200 perturbed re-extracts.

## Corrections this panel forced on the seat's own dispatch

1. **NOT "14 five-year bands".** Twelve members are five-year (15-19 … 70-74); `75 to 84 years` is a
   TEN-year member and `85 years and over` is open-ended. **The published granularity degrades exactly
   where the supply side lives**, and every method that assumes uniform panels breaks on it — which is
   why all three proposals independently NAMED AND REFUSED Sprague/Beers/Karup-King-Newton.
2. **Base-year OwnerStock IS shape-invariant among member-closed curves** (the constraint map said
   otherwise). All finalists give exactly **2,227,280** vs 2,179,183.37 committed (+2.207%), and the
   arithmetic is a one-glance check: `2,245,600 − 1,150 − 17,170`. **This is the cheapest simultaneous
   validation of any implementation's closure** — use it.

## What the committed curve is actually asserting

**8,304 phantom households maintained by someone aged 0-14**, and 15-19 understated by **4.17x**. The
repair is inert today (every sub-25 term is multiplied by `ownership(a) = 0`) and goes live at the ordered
second step — spec §7 amendment #12 puts this curve BEFORE any downward move of the ownership floor.

## The success criterion, measured

Largest single-age share of `D_native`: committed **65-77% (age 35)** → naive 14-member step curve
**44-50% (age 25)** → all three finalists **14-18%**. HHI 0.45-0.62 → 0.075-0.092. **The naive step curve
is confirmed the worst design available** — consuming the granularity without a shape rule makes it worse,
which is the minimal-change lens earning its slot by refuting itself.

## Two defects the judge found in the proposals' own text

- **P2's proposed legacy-band gate fails on P2's own curve** (1e-15 relative; actual 3.99e-15). A gate that
  cannot pass its own construction is worse than no gate.
- **P1's terminal end-tangent rule is under-specified**, and the fix improves the curve: under the
  three-point rule its prose specifies, the "dips at 90 then RISES at 100, demographically backwards"

3. **The fc/fb arm-adequacy finding was measured at the same probe fidelity.** If the P_resident scale compresses the D_native leg differentially across geographies, the arm spread could shrink relative to the family spread and a third, genuinely different construction (P2's rate-space PCHIP, or P3's piecewise-linear) might be needed as the second sweep endpoint. **Re-measure the arm spread on the live pipeline before trusting `rank_stable` under it.**

7. **The 74/75 osculatory overshoot is the winner's one real curve defect** and I have not measured its isolated contribution to the ranked quantity — only that it lives entirely in OwnerStock and that OwnerStock's shape sensitivity at projected years is 0.03–0.065%, i.e. small. 8. **My reimplementations are mine, not the proposals'.** P2 and P3 reproduced exactly; P1's tail did not, and I resolved that in P1's favour on the evidence that its own prose specifies the rule I used. If the P1 author intended the plain-secant end, my recommended end rule is a *change* to their design, not a clarification of it.

## 4. RECOMMENDATION

### Build **P1's chassis** — exposure-abscissa monotone cumulative Hermite — with the terminal rule pinned, and graft P2's and P3's gate discipline onto it.

**Why P1 wins, on the one criterion the task names as decisive.** The task says a method that claims mean-preservation and does not have it is the worst outcome available. P1's closure is **algebraic, not numerical**: the member endpoints *are* interpolation knots, so `Σ_{a∈m} P·h = Y(X(hi)) − Y(X(lo−1)) = M_m` telescopes exactly, **independent of the tangent rule, the refactor, the seed, or whether any iteration converged**. Measured 0.0 on all 14 members under both tangent arms and across 200 perturbed re-extracts. P2 and P3 conserve today at 1e-11 because their solves converged today; P1 conserves because it cannot not. It also has no iteration, no seed and no convergence gate, so byte-reproducibility is structural rather than pinned; and its abscissa choice is *measured*, not aesthetic (§4.1).

**What P1 gives up, stated plainly.** Its peak sits at age 77 at 0.66202, **2.2% above the member-rate hull** — a genuine osculatory overshoot where the interval width changes 5→10 *and* the rate reverses. P2 is the only curve without it (peak 72, matching the published peak member 70-74). The overshoot touches **only** OwnerStock (`AGE_BOUNDARY = 75`), is ≤ 1 by certificate, and cannot be removed without either dropping to the refuted 14-step curve or adding an unanchored constraint (P1 measured a hull clamp producing `h(15) = −0.0061`). **Record it and pin it by test; do not paper over it.**

**4.1 Verified anchor for the abscissa assumption.** P1's justification is empirical and it holds: on the **age** abscissa (DemoTools `graduate_mono` as published), the Fritsch-Butland arm reaches **max h = 1.1214 at age 98 — out of range**, and the Fritsch-Carlson arm produces a spurious **−0.0217 step at age 56** (`h(55) = 0.6231 → h(56) = 0.6015`) driven purely by `P(56)/P(55) = 1.0824`, a real cohort bulge. Both figures are P1's, reproduced exactly by me — and `1.1214` is *the same number P2 independently quotes for its own refuted cumulative-count route*, so the two proposals refuted the same construction and agree on its magnitude. **Caveat: I could NOT reproduce P1's `h(100) = 2.059`** — it is knot/evaluation-convention dependent. The generator must record its *own computed* refutation figures, never transcribe 2.059.

## 2. THE CONSERVATION CONTRACT, AS AN EQUATION

Let `P(a)` = the committed base-year ISQ denominator (Le Québec / Référence (A2026) / 2021 / status `r` / sex code 3), `a ∈ [0,100]`, with `P(100)` the 100+ lump. Let `M_m` = the published `Tenure (4):Total - Tenure[1]` count at `GEO=Quebec` for maintainer-age member `m`, every non-age dimension at its `Total -` member. Let 𝕄 = the 14 members with spans `(15,19) (20,24) (25,29) (30,34) (35,39) (40,44) (45,49) (50,54) (55,59) (60,64) (65,69) (70,74) (75,84) (85,100)`.

```
C1  (per-member closure — THE contract)
      ∀ m ∈ 𝕄 :   Σ_{a=lo_m}^{hi_m} P(a)·h(a)  =  M_m

C2  (zero floor)          h(a) = 0.0  exactly, ∀ a ∈ [0,14]

C3  (legacy identity, IMPLIED)   C1 ∧ C2  ⟹  for each of the six current bands B,
      Σ_{a∈B} P(a)·h(a) = Σ_{m⊂B} M_m   exactly

C4  (range)               0 ≤ h(a) ≤ 1  ∀ a, BY CONSTRUCTION
C5  (full support)        h(a) defined at every integer a ∈ [0,100]
C6  (under-determination) within-member shape is UNIDENTIFIED by these two sources
```

**Tolerance.** Two different numbers, and conflating them is a design error.
- *Achievable*: C1 is one linear constraint over 5 (or 10, or 16) free values per member, so it is solvable **exactly**. A construction that satisfies C1 should be asserted at float epsilon (rel 1e-12), not at 2.5.
- *Admissible*: `M_m` is round-to-5, `P(a)` is not (`headship_by_age.json._provenance.rounding_note`). So the honest bound a *re-extract* must survive is `|residual_m| ≤ 2.5` per member, and the aggregate bound stays the existing `2.5 × (14 + 1) = 37.5` (`census.py:842-849`). No correction is applied to either side — that stays.

**Why C3 is the crux for any proposal.** Every one of the six current bands is an **exact union of published members**: `0-19 = {15-19}`, `20-34 = {20-24, 25-29, 30-34}`, `35-54` = 4 members, `55-64` = 2, `65-74` = 2, `75+ = {75-84, 85+}`. So a curve satisfying C1+C2 reproduces the existing `band_maintainers`, `band_persons` and the `+5` `numerator_closure` **unchanged and byte-identical**. The C2 clause is required, not decorative: the `0-19` band's denominator spans ages 0-19 while its only member spans 15-19, so the band identity needs the zero floor as well as the member closure. **C1 is strictly stronger than what the current artifact proves. Aggregate consistency is not "preserved carefully" — it is subsumed.** A proposal that argues it "protects" the aggregate has not understood the constraint.

**C6 is the honesty clause.** C1 pins one linear functional per member and leaves 4, 9 or 15 degrees of freedom. Any single-year curve is *published aggregate + declared shape assumption*. In a package whose charter is "derivation, never transcription" (`census.py:7-13`) and "every constant carries its documented anchor" (`census.py:43-47`), the shape choice is a new constant, not an implementation detail. See F6.

**Scope warning.** C1 is a **base-year, QC-level, both-sexes** identity. It holds at no projected year, no CMA geography, and no other scenario. A finer curve makes a use-site conservation gate look attractive; such a gate would be false. See F14.

## 4. THE 0-19 PROBLEM

**What the current band rate actually asserts** (computed from committed bytes):
- `h = 10,920 / 1,794,061 = 0.0060867495586828` applied flat to ages 0..19.

**Is it observable today? ## 5. THE 75-84 / 85+ TAIL

**Published shape (computed):** 65-69 `0.633739`, 70-74 `0.647620`, **75-84 `0.630033`, 85+ `0.494789`**. The true curve **peaks at 70-74 and declines through the tail**. **Granularity asymmetry.** The finest available resolution here is 10-year and open-ended, against 5-year everywhere else. So the tail carries more shape assumption per unit of published constraint than any other part of the curve — the exact opposite of the supply side's needs.

**Denominator asymmetry.** The numerator (`85 years and over`) is genuinely open-ended; the ISQ denominator is **closed at 100 with the 100+ lump at age 100** (`isq_ages.py`, `_POP_TERMINAL_AGE = 100` at `census.py:186`). Σ_{85..100} P(a) = 209,463. They match only because both open ends coincide. Do not extend the curve past 100.

**D_native never reaches here.** `formation.AGE_BOUNDARY = 75` (`formation.py:52`), loop `range(19, 75)` (`formation.py:209`). The tail touches **only** OwnerStock — the ED denominator.

**No new coupling to the supply side, and none may be created.** The 75+ cohort machinery reads `living_arrangement` + ownership, never headship (`pipeline.py:679-694`). Two adjacent person→household maps at the same ages, deliberately not unified, with opposite multiplicand rules.

**But there is a real, time-varying consequence on the ranked quantity.** Ownership is a single flat `75+` band, so `OwnerStock_75+ = own_75+ × Σ_{a≥75} P(a)·h(a)`. Under a flat `h` that term is proportional to total 75+ population; under a resolved tail it is not — and the 85+ share of 75+ rises from **28.19% (2021) to 41.78% (2051)** in the committed QC Référence fan.

ED is a ratio, so this is a **year-varying, geography-varying multiplicative shift on the ranked quantity** (each geography has its own 75+ age structure and its own ownership rate). It cannot be dismissed as a level shift, and it is the mechanism by which the tail can reorder the ranking despite never entering D.

## 6. THE AGE-20 INTERACTION AND THE SUB-25 OWNERSHIP ZERO

### The headline consequence: the fix makes D_native BIGGER, not smaller

## 7. WHAT WOULD MAKE A PROPOSAL WRONG — falsifiers for a judge

Any single failure is disqualifying.

**F1 — Member closure.** Does it reproduce all **14** published member counts against the committed single-year denominator, and does the *generator* **prove** it per member (computed residual, raising above tolerance) rather than assert it? A construction conserving only the six legacy bands has not used the granularity that exists and is a non-fix.

**F2 — Under-15 zero.** Is `h(a) = 0.0` exactly for `a ≤ 14`, justified by the dimension's declared cardinality plus the closure bound (unpublished mass ≤ 35 households, rate < 2.6 × 10⁻⁵), and stated as *"closure pins under-15 maintainers below rounding scale, so 0 is the only admissible value"* — never as "the table is silent" and never as "proved exactly zero"? **F3 — Legacy provenance reproduced.** Do `band_persons`, `band_maintainers`, and `numerator_closure`'s `+5` delta against 3,749,035 at tolerance 37.5 remain **derivable and unchanged** from the new curve? Deleting them to avoid a mismatch is refuted.

**F4 — Piecewise-constant at member scale is the defect relocated, not fixed.** Ruling V says *genuine age-resolved*. **F5 — Non-monotone tail.** Does the design permit `h(a) < h(a−1)`? A globally monotone construction is refuted by `70-74 (0.6476) → 75-84 (0.6300) → 85+ (0.4948)`.

**F6 — Range by construction, and shape declared as an assumption.** Is `0 ≤ h ≤ 1` provable (shape-preserving interpolants live in the convex hull of the member rates; unconstrained splines do not), rather than merely `assert_fraction`ed at load?
